Ethan Torchio

Profile

• Full Name: Ethan Torchio • Date of Birth: October 8, 2000 • Birthplace: Rome / Frosinone, Italy • Height: 178 cm • Position: Drummer

Biography & Musical Background

Ethan Torchio was born in Rome and spent part of his childhood in the province of Frosinone. Growing up in a large, artistic family—his father is a documentary film director—he was drawn to rhythm and music from a very early age. Around the age of 13, he started experimenting with electronic music production using Nuendo, before finding his true passion behind a drum kit. His biggest musical inspiration came from Stewart Copeland, the legendary drummer of The Police, whom he discovered through an old CD.

Career

Ethan officially debuted with Måneskin in 2017 with their EP Chosen, shortly after the band gained nationwide fame by finishing second on the eleventh season of X Factor Italy. Over the next few years, his precise, steady, and dynamic drumming became a defining pillar of the band's sound across their studio albums: Il ballo della vita (2018), Teatro d'ira - Vol. I (2021), and RUSH! (2023). In 2021, alongside his bandmates, he won both the Sanremo Music Festival and the Eurovision Song Contest with "Zitti e buoni," propelling Måneskin to global stardom. That same year, his talent and impact as a musician were officially recognized when he was named a global artist for the renowned drum brand Ludwig Drums.

Solo Activity & Life During the Band's Hiatus

Following the conclusion of the RUSH! World Tour in 2024, Måneskin entered an indefinite break so the members could explore personal and individual projects. While his bandmates stepped into the spotlight with solo releases and tours, Ethan—true to his naturally introverted and thoughtful personality—chose to step away from public attention and maintain a very low profile. During this quiet period out of the limelight, he kept his solo output minimal, focusing on personal life and subtle behind-the-scenes projects, most notably composing the drum soundtrack for the 2024 mental health documentary Ogni Pensiero Vola.

Behind the Drums

• The Quiet Anchor: Unlike the loud, extroverted energy of his bandmates on stage, Ethan is known for being calm, reserved, and deeply focused during interviews and performances.

• Signature Look: He has maintained his signature long, dark hair since childhood and is rarely seen without it tied back or flowing during live shows.

• Personal Identity: He has openly described his orientation as "sexually free," reflecting the band's overall message of self-expression and breaking stereotypes
