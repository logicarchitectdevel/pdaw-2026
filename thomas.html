Thomas Raggi

Profile

Thomas Raggi is an Italian guitarist and songwriter, best known as the lead guitarist of the rock band Måneskin. Recognized for his energetic performances, distinctive guitar riffs, and passion for classic rock, he plays an essential role in shaping the band's musical identity.

Biography & Musical Background

Thomas Raggi was born on January 18, 2001, in Rome, Italy. His interest in music developed at a young age, and he began playing guitar as a child. Growing up, he became particularly interested in rock music and guitar legends such as Jimi Hendrix, Slash, Jimmy Page, and John Frusciante.

During his school years, Thomas met Victoria De Angelis. The two shared a passion for music and eventually decided to form a band. Their musical project gradually developed with the arrival of Damiano David and Ethan Torchio, completing the lineup of Måneskin.

Career

Måneskin began their journey by performing on the streets of Rome and sharing cover performances online. In 2017, the band participated in the Italian television competition X Factor, where they finished in second place.

Their first studio album, Il ballo della vita, was released in 2018, followed by Teatro d'ira: Vol. I in 2021. The band's international breakthrough came after winning the Sanremo Music Festival and Eurovision Song Contest in 2021 with the song "Zitti e buoni".

As the band's guitarist, Thomas became known for his powerful riffs, expressive solos, and ability to combine different rock influences. His playing contributed significantly to the sound of songs such as "Zitti e buoni", "I Wanna Be Your Slave", and "Gossip".

Solo Activity & Masquerade

Alongside his work with Måneskin, Thomas began exploring new musical directions and collaborations outside the band. His interest in different styles and his desire to experiment eventually led him to develop his own solo project.

In December 2025, he released his debut solo album, Masquerade. The project brought together several internationally recognized musicians, including Tom Morello, Chad Smith, Serge Pizzorno, and Alex Kapranos.

Built around guitar-driven compositions, the album explores different rock influences and musical styles. Thomas described the project as an opportunity to bring together musicians from different generations and experiment with new creative ideas.

Behind the Guitar

Thomas draws inspiration from a wide range of guitarists, particularly those associated with classic rock and alternative music. Among his influences are Jimi Hendrix, Jimmy Page, Slash, and John Frusciante.

His approach to music combines instinct, experimentation, and a strong focus on guitar sound. Whether performing with Måneskin or working on his solo material, Thomas continues to explore new ways of expressing his musical identity.
