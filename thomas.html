Thomas Raggi

Profile

Thomas Raggi is an Italian musician best known as the lead guitarist of the rock band Måneskin. Recognized for his energetic performances, distinctive guitar sound and passion for rock music, he plays an important role in shaping the band's musical identity.

Biography & Musical Background

Thomas Raggi was born on January 18, 2001, in Rome, Italy. From an early age, he developed a strong interest in music and began learning to play the guitar. His passion for rock music influenced his musical development and helped him discover his own style.

During his school years, he met Damiano David, Victoria De Angelis and Ethan Torchio. Together, they formed Måneskin in 2016, beginning a musical journey that would eventually take them onto the international stage.

Career

Måneskin gained national attention after participating in the Italian television competition X Factor in 2017, where the band finished in second place.

Their major breakthrough came in 2021, when they won the Sanremo Music Festival with "Zitti e buoni" and later represented Italy at the Eurovision Song Contest, achieving first place.

With albums such as Teatro d'ira Vol. I and Rush!, Måneskin reached audiences around the world and established themselves as one of the most recognizable contemporary rock bands.

Musical Style & Influences

Thomas is known for his expressive guitar playing, powerful riffs and energetic stage presence. His style combines elements of classic rock, hard rock and modern alternative music.

His guitar work contributes to the band's dynamic sound, adding intensity and character to both their studio recordings and live performances.

Behind the Guitar

Interestingly, his main guitar is a Japanese Squier given to him by his first guitar teacher. As Måneskin's only guitarist, Thomas takes responsibility for both the rhythm and lead parts, combining riffs, melodies and solos to create the band's sound.

Thomas developed his passion for rock music at an early age, influenced by his father's vinyl collection, which included bands such as Metallica, Skid Row and Anthrax.
