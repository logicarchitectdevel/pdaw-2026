Victoria De Angelis

Profile

Victoria De Angelis, often affectionately called Vic, is an Italian musician, songwriter, producer, and DJ, best known as the bassist of the rock band Måneskin. Recognized for her energetic stage presence, distinctive style, and powerful bass performances, she has become one of the defining personalities of the group.

Biography & Musical Background

Victoria De Angelis was born on April 28, 2000, in Rome, Italy. Growing up in a multicultural family with Italian and Danish roots, she developed an interest in music at an early age. She began playing guitar when she was eight years old and later discovered the bass guitar, which became her main instrument.

During her school years, Victoria met guitarist Thomas Raggi. Their shared passion for music eventually led them to form a band. Damiano David later joined as vocalist, while Ethan Torchio completed the lineup as drummer.

The band's name, Måneskin, meaning "moonlight" in Danish, was suggested by Victoria during the process of choosing a name for a local music competition.

Career

Before achieving international recognition, Victoria and the other members of Måneskin performed as street musicians in Rome. Their first major breakthrough came in 2017, when they participated in the Italian television competition X Factor, finishing in second place.

The band's popularity continued to grow with the release of their debut album, Il ballo della vita, in 2018. Their international breakthrough arrived in 2021, when Måneskin won the Sanremo Music Festival with "Zitti e buoni" and subsequently represented Italy at the Eurovision Song Contest, where they also claimed first place.

As the band's bassist, Victoria became known for her energetic performances and distinctive musical style. Her stage presence and individuality helped shape Måneskin's visual and artistic identity.

Solo Activity & Electronic Music

Alongside her work with Måneskin, Victoria developed a growing interest in electronic music and DJing. She occasionally performed DJ sets at afterparties following the band's concerts, gradually turning this interest into a separate musical project.

In 2024, she officially launched her solo DJ career, performing at clubs and events in different countries. On August 30, 2024, she released her debut solo single, "GET UP BITCH! Shake ya ass", featuring Brazilian singer Anitta.

Her solo sound explores electronic music, techno, Baile Funk, and Latin club influences, revealing a different side of her musical identity.

Behind the Scenes

Beyond her role as a bassist, Victoria is known for her creative individuality and interest in fashion, art, and different musical cultures. Her artistic expression goes beyond the traditional boundaries of rock music.

Whether performing on stage with Måneskin or behind the DJ decks, Victoria continues to explore new sounds and creative directions while maintaining the individuality that has always defined her.
