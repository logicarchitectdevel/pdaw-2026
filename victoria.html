Biography & Musical Background

Victoria De Angelis was born on April 28, 2000, in Rome, Italy. She developed an interest in music at a young age and began playing the bass guitar during her teenage years. Her passion for rock music and her distinctive musical style became important elements of her artistic identity.

During her school years, Victoria met Thomas Raggi. Their shared interest in music eventually led them to form a band. Together with Damiano David and Ethan Torchio, they created Måneskin, beginning a musical journey that would take them from local performances to international stages.

Career

Victoria co-founded Måneskin in 2016 alongside Damiano David, Thomas Raggi, and Ethan Torchio. The band initially performed on the streets of Rome before gaining wider recognition through their participation in the eleventh season of X Factor Italy in 2017, where they finished in second place.

In 2021, Måneskin achieved international success after winning the Sanremo Music Festival with "Zitti e buoni" and subsequently winning the Eurovision Song Contest. Their success brought the band worldwide recognition, leading to international tours, major festival appearances, and the release of albums such as Teatro d'ira: Vol. I and Rush!

Musical Style & Influences

Victoria's bass playing is an essential part of Måneskin's sound. Her performances combine rhythmic precision with the energy and attitude associated with rock music.

Her musical influences include rock, alternative music, and punk. These styles have contributed to the band's distinctive sound and energetic stage performances.
