Biography & Musical Background

Damiano David was born on January 8, 1999, in Rome, Italy, to parents Daniele David and Rossella Scognamiglio, both flight attendants. Their profession allowed him to travel around the world from a young age, exposing him to different cultures and experiences. During his teenage years, he developed an interest in music and performance, eventually discovering his passion for singing. His charismatic personality and distinctive vocal style would later become central to Måneskin's identity.

Career

Damiano's musical career began with Måneskin, the Italian rock band formed in Rome alongside Victoria De Angelis, Thomas Raggi, and Ethan Torchio. In 2017, the group gained national recognition after finishing second in the eleventh season of X Factor Italy. Their debut studio album, Il ballo della vita, was released in 2018 and marked an important step in their growing popularity.

In 2021, Måneskin achieved international success after winning the Sanremo Music Festival and the Eurovision Song Contest with the song "Zitti e buoni." The victory established Damiano as one of the most recognizable rock vocalists of his generation. The band's success continued with the release of Teatro d'ira: Vol. I in 2021 and Rush! in 2023, followed by international tours and performances at major music festivals.

Solo Activity & Life During the Band's Hiatus

In 2024, Damiano began exploring his artistic identity outside Måneskin. He launched his solo career with the single "Silverlines," produced by Labrinth, followed by "Born With a Broken Heart." These releases introduced a more personal and emotionally expressive side of his music, moving away from the band's characteristic rock sound.

In May 2025, he released his debut solo album, Funny Little Fears. The record explored themes of vulnerability, anxiety, personal growth, and self-discovery. Through this project, Damiano demonstrated his versatility as a singer-songwriter and began a new chapter in his career while remaining associated with Måneskin.

Behind the Microphone
